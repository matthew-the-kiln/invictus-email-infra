Move 3

Google Workspace connects in Cloudflare.

The first DNS records come from Google Workspace. They are added in Cloudflare, not GoDaddy.

Type
Record
Purpose
TXT
@google-site-verification
google-site-verification=<value from Google>Proves the domain belongs to the client (authorization).
MX
@Mail routing
1 smtp.google.comSends incoming email to Google, so the mailboxes can receive replies. Priority 1.
TXT
@SPF
v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~allLists Google as an allowed sender.
TXT
google._domainkeyDKIM
v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=<key from Google Admin>Signs outgoing mail so it can be verified.

Values shown are the standard format. The real verification and DKIM values are generated in Google Workspace. Google also supports an older set of five MX records.

Next steps: connect Instantly

Allow Instantly in your Google Workspace.

A Workspace admin approves Instantly. After that, mailboxes can be connected to Instantly with Google sign-in.

  1. Open the Google Workspace Admin Panel

    Go to Configured apps in the Admin Console and sign in as a Workspace admin.

  2. Click "Configure new app"

    This opens the app search.

  3. Search for Instantly with this Client ID

    536726988839-pt93oro4685dtb1emb0pp2vjgjol5mls.apps.googleusercontent.com

  4. Select and approve Instantly

    Approve access to your Google Workspace.

  5. Add the accounts and inboxes

    Back in Instantly, click Login, then click the Google accounts you want to add.
